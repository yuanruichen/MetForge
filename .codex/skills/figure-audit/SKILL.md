# Figure Audit

Use this skill after creating or revising a scientific figure.

## Check

- Variable name and units are visible.
- Period, season, or sample definition is visible.
- Method tag is visible when relevant.
- Axes, colorbars, legends, and labels are readable.
- Multi-panel spacing is balanced.
- Text does not overlap plotted data or other labels.
- Color choices are interpretable and accessible.
- PNG and PDF outputs exist when requested.

## Output

Give a score out of 100 and list the most important fixes first. If the score
is below the requested threshold, revise the figure before delivery.
