# Literature Review

Use this skill for focused scientific literature synthesis.

## Instructions

- Start from the research question.
- Prefer primary sources and official datasets/documentation.
- Track citation metadata as you work.
- Separate source claims from your interpretation.
- Compare methods, assumptions, variables, periods, and limitations.
- Identify gaps that connect directly to the user's question.

## Output

Provide:
- a concise synthesis
- key papers or sources
- points of agreement and disagreement
- open gaps or next reading targets
- stable links or DOIs when available
